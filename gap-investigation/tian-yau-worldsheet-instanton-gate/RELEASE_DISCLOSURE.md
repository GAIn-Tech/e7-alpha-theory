# Release disclosure

## Established by exact computation and algebra

* The explicit Fermat/bilinear cover has 81 `(1,0)`, 81 `(0,1)`, and 567 `(1,1)` isolated rational curves in the searched parameterizations.
* The `(1,1)` line-pair classification is exhaustive: 567 rank-two pairs, 162 rank-one pairs, no rank-zero pairs/families.
* The free `Z3` action partitions them into 27, 27, and 189 length-three orbits.
* Every isolated curve has `N_{C/X}=O(-1)²` and `TX|C=O(2)⊕O(-1)²`.
* For `V=TQ`, `h0(C,V|C⊗O(-1))=2`, so every listed Pfaffian vanishes. The low-degree worldsheet coefficients are exactly zero, not fitted.

## External inputs

The complete-intersection geometry and diagonal action are taken from Butbaia et al. The worldsheet Pfaffian zero-mode criterion is taken from Witten. Standard exact facts about Fermat cubic lines, vector bundles on `P1`, and étale tangent pullback are used in the written derivation.

## Not established

* No determinant magnitude is assigned.
* No singular-curve or multiple-cover sector is computed.
* Total bidegree three is the next unenumerated degree, but ordinary smooth isolated curves there remain obstructed by the same standard-embedding tangent zero modes.
* No nonzero worldsheet term, stabilized critical point, threshold completion, alpha prediction, or publication claim is made.
* Verification is local and not independent peer review.
