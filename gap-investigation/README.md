## Claim boundary (fixed before checking)

We test a stated class of four-dimensional gauge effective actions with fixed compact E7 algebra, fixed generator/charge normalization, conventional quadratic gauge kinetic term and independent positive kinetic coefficient. The invariance of the unscaled kinetic functional is an explicit mathematical assumption, not a newly formalized E7 theorem. We test whether rescaling its coefficient preserves invariance, whether a positive continuous family survives simple invariant relations and one-loop affine inverse-coupling running/matching, and whether fixing an independent boundary condition restores uniqueness.

This is a conditional non-identifiability result for that model class, not a no-go theorem for all UV completions or a proof about quantum-gravity vacuum selection. A UV completion might fix the boundary coupling dynamically; that requires an explicit action, spectrum, vacuum, matching and a calculation. A unique group invariant form up to scale does not itself provide that scale.

No experimental measurement is inserted into the symbolic identifiability constraints. Arithmetic discrepancies are separately compared with the user-provided CODATA 2022 value 137.035999177 and quoted standard uncertainty 0.000000021, not a newly retrieved measurement. No model uncertainty is invented.
