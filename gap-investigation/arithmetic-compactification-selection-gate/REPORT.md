## Verdict

The strongest surviving law is a **consistency law**, not a selection law:

`I_cover=d I_quotient`, and for a tangent standard embedding on a CY threefold, `I_cover=Euler_cover/2`; therefore `|net|=3` exactly requires `|Euler_cover|=6d` in the stated convention. This follows only after choosing a ten-dimensional heterotic theory, a six-real-dimensional CY geometry, a free degree-`d` action, an equivariant bundle, integral flux/Chern data, Bianchi/HYM consistency and a modular completion. It does not choose any of those inputs.

The preregistered finite test rejects prime-order necessity and sufficiency. All four catalog models have four external dimensions because a CY threefold was supplied as an input. No inspected primary source derives a partition, nimber, Ramanujan, Dirac-large-number or Dedekind-number predicate that selects that input or net three. This is a bounded non-identifiability result, not a universal physics no-go.

## Preregistered physical map

1. `D_external=10-dim_R(X)=4`; this is dimensional bookkeeping after choosing `X6`.

5. Full modular invariance/level matching, not one E7 character.

The earlier modular and knot gates show why eta/Ramanujan differential identities can constrain characters or charge-refined threshold traces while plain E7 `S,T`, link data, and partition congruences do not determine geometry, family index or thresholds.
