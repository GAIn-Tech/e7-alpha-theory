# Primary-source evidence notes

[3] Exceptional Reductions, arXiv:1012.5797, printed p.12/PDF p.13, Eqs.(4.2)-(4.3), visually verified overbars in exceptional-branching-page13.png:
133 -> 78_0 + 27_{-2} + conjugate(27)_{+2} + 1_0.
56 -> 27_{+1} + conjugate(27)_{-1} + 1_{+3} + 1_{-3}.
The unbarred 27 has -2 in 133 but +1 in 56 in this consistent convention. Reversing ALL U1 charges is a generator convention (algebraic observation); do not independently flip one equation.

[8] Magic Coset Decompositions, arXiv:1201.6314, printed p.19/PDF p.20, Eqs.(4.41)-(4.42): 27 -> 1_{+4}+10_{-2}+16_{+1}, with conjugate sector carrying reversed charges and conjugate 16. This paper discusses E7(-25), compact E6(-78), and an alternative noncompact chain: these representation branchings do not identify the real forms or construct a chiral vacuum.

[4] King, Moretti, Nevzorov, hep-ph/0511256, printed pp.1-3/PDF pp.2-4: E6 -> SO10 x U1_psi; SO10 -> SU5 x U1_chi. Eq.(2): 27 -> (10,1)+(5bar,2)+(5bar,-3)+(5,-2)+(1,5)+(1,0) under SU5 x U1_N. Eq.(1): normalized Q_N=Q_chi/4+sqrt(15)Q_psi/4. Integer labels in Eq.(2) are sqrt(40)Q_N (Table 1). U1_N is not the E7/E6 U1 and not U1_psi or U1_chi individually. Ordinary SM matter occupies 10+5bar and N^c is the neutral singlet; extra 5+5bar provides vectorlike SM exotics and Higgs-type doublets, plus another singlet.
The uncharged standard matter chain combining these identifications is 27 -> 16+10+1 -> (10+5bar+1)+(5+5bar)+1. Individual integer U1_chi signs were NOT independently verified here; do not attribute them to checked Slansky tables.

[4] Table 1/PDF p.4: with left-handed Weyl/charge-conjugate notation and Qem=T3+Y, the family is Q=(3,2,1/6), u^c=(3bar,1,-2/3), d^c=(3bar,1,1/3), L=(1,2,-1/2), e^c=(1,1,1), N^c=(1,1,0). The table uses normalized Q^Y with sqrt(5/3)Q^Y=Y. Witten [1], printed p.3/PDF p.2, uses double these hypercharges; that is normalization, not disagreement.

[1] Witten, Fermion Quantum Numbers in Kaluza-Klein Theory (October 1983), abstract/PDF p.1: absent elementary gauge fields, the Atiyah-Hirzebruch theorem makes the higher-dimensional Dirac reduction vectorlike in four dimensions. The Rarita-Schwinger result is proved on homogeneous spaces, not as a general impossibility theorem. Do not extend this to all bundles, orbifolds, compactifications or higher-spin fields. Printed pp.3-4/PDF p.2: fixed-helicity SM fermions transform in a complex representation, with gauge invariance forbidding bare masses.

[5] Kawamura, hep-ph/0012125, Sections 2-3: odd-parity modes have no zero modes on S1/(Z2 x Z2prime); explicit parity assignments reduce symmetry. Crucially this model ASSUMES three chiral families 3(5bar+10) on the y=0 wall. It does not derive families by projecting E7. It is primary evidence for a concrete projection mechanism, not an E7 chirality existence proof.

Inference / next research gate: the conjugate pairs in [3] do not themselves select net SM chirality. Specify the higher-dimensional fermion representation and spacetime chirality, compactification/bundle or boundary/parity data, surviving gauge group, and compute representation-resolved zero-mode imbalance n(R)-n(Rbar). If three families are claimed, show three net copies of the whole SM family, with mirror/exotic treatment and anomaly consistency. This is an acceptance requirement inferred from the sources, not a completed compactification.
