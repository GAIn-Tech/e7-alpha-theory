# Source receipts

1. Cicoli, de Alwis, Westphal, *Heterotic Moduli Stabilisation*, arXiv:1304.1809v3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.1809 — equations (2.2)–(2.4), (2.23), section 2.2.3 and sections 3.2.1–3.2.2; also explicitly states nonzero H in an N=1 solution makes the internal geometry non-Kähler and that threshold-only supersymmetric extrema can be uncontrolled while a worldsheet term can repair that in suitable models.
2. Butbaia et al., *Physical Yukawa Couplings in Heterotic String Compactifications*, arXiv:2401.15078, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15078 — actual Fermat/bilinear Tian–Yau member, free diagonal Z3, tangent standard embedding and quotient modes.
3. Candelas, de la Ossa, He, Szendrői, *Triadophilia*, arXiv:0706.3134, https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/szendroi/atmp_triadophilia.pdf — quotient topology and Hodge data.
4. Curio, Krause, Lüst, *Moduli stabilization in the heterotic/IIB discretuum*, hep-th/0502168, https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/0502168 — primary mechanism combining hidden condensation, threshold dependence, and a worldsheet instanton in heterotic compactifications.
5. Strominger, *Superstrings with Torsion*, Nucl. Phys. B274 (1986) 253, DOI:10.1016/0550-3213(86)90286-5 — N=1 heterotic torsion system; used only to classify nonzero H as outside the retained Kähler CY ansatz.
6. Witten, *Non-Perturbative Superpotentials in String Theory*, hep-th/9604030 / Nucl. Phys. B474 (1996) 343, https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9604030 — worldsheet-instanton Pfaffian/zero-mode criterion.
