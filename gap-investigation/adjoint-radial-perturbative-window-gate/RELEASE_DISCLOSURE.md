# Claim boundary

Epsilon, M and finite matching remain free action inputs. Analysis tolerances are explicitly chosen diagnostics, not measured quantities, a fit to alpha, or new physics parameters.

A finite one-loop coefficient is not a controlled pole. We check its relative size, the stationary displacement, momentum derivative, and active threshold and eigenvalue separation. The strict-Landau derivative is gauge dependent. General-xi on-shell cancellation does not imply derivative cancellation or a uniform threshold-free gauge domain.

All 188 scalars, including 67 gauge Goldstones and 11 physical tree flats, and 79 vectors are retained in action data. Hard-mode vacuum continuation is not a full resummed stable quantum vacuum. Conditional necessary smallness bounds do not bound unknown higher orders, finite matching or off-diagonal self-energies. Imaginary cuts are retained, but no mass or physical width is inferred.

Numerical action tensors are double precision; high-precision integration is not an interval or exact tensor certificate.
